# Privacy Policy — ArcTag NFC

**Last updated:** July 24, 2026  
**App:** ArcTag NFC (`com.arctagnfc.app`)  
**Contact:** privacy@arctagnfc.example

## Summary
ArcTag NFC helps you write and read NFC tags using simple templates. We collect as little personal data as possible.

## Data we collect
In the current release:
- **No account is required.**
- **No analytics SDKs** are bundled by default.
- Tag contents you write (Wi‑Fi credentials, URLs, notes, contacts) are written to **your NFC tags** and may be stored locally on device as saved profiles. We do not operate a cloud sync service by default.

## Permissions
- **NFC** — required to read and write NFC tags on Android.

## Sensitive content warning
If you write Wi‑Fi passwords or personal contact info to a tag, anyone who can tap that tag may read it. Treat physical tags like shared notes.

## Data sharing
We do not sell personal data.

## Children
ArcTag NFC is not directed at children under 13.
