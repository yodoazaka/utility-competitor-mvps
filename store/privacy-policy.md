# Privacy Policy — Apex OBD

**Last updated:** July 24, 2026  
**App:** Apex OBD (`com.apexobd.app`)  
**Contact:** privacy@apexobd.example

## Summary
Apex OBD helps you view vehicle diagnostic data via a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter. We design the app to collect as little personal data as possible.

## Data we collect
In the current release:
- **No account is required.**
- **No analytics SDKs** are bundled by default.
- **No advertising ID** is collected.
- Vehicle readings (RPM, fault codes, etc.) are processed **on your device** for display. We do not operate a server that stores your vehicle PIDs by default.

## Permissions
- **Bluetooth / Nearby devices** — to discover and connect to your OBD-II adapter.
- **Location (Android)** — required by Android on some OS versions to scan for Bluetooth devices. We do not use GPS to track your location for advertising or profiling.

## Demo mode
Demo mode uses sample data only. No vehicle connection is made.

## Data sharing
We do not sell personal data. We do not share vehicle diagnostic data with third parties unless you explicitly use a future feature that requires it (with notice).

## Children
Apex OBD is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices
Uninstall the app to remove local data. Revoke Bluetooth/Location permissions in Android Settings at any time.

## Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will bump the “Last updated” date in-app and here.
