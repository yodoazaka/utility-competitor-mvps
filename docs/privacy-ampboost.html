Privacy Policy — AmpBoost

Last updated: July 25, 2026 · App: AmpBoost (com.ampboost.app)

Summary

AmpBoost is a free session helper for mobile gaming. It is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

Data we collect

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics SDKs are bundled by default.
  • No advertising ID is collected.
  • Optional tip purchases (when enabled via Google Play Billing) are processed by Google; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Permissions

This release requests no sensitive permissions by default. Future versions that trim background apps may request additional Android capabilities with in-app explanation.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Children

AmpBoost is not directed at children under 13.
